Data Methodology & Architecture
Data is sourced from the UN OCHA Financial Tracking Service (FTS) API.
• Macro Layer: Aggregates capital flows directed to large UN Agencies and inter-governmental pooled funds.
• Micro Layer: Explicitly filters the FTS database for downstream recipients categorized as Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs).
Note: FTS relies on voluntary reporting. Some downstream implementing partner data may be delayed or anonymized due to security concerns.
